Scroll to drive the camera

viewBox camera

The background is one fixed SVG. As you scroll, its viewBox is interpolated between per-section waypoints, so the map pans and zooms. Keep scrolling — watch the grid and the beacon.

Section 02

Zoom out to the full extent

This waypoint frames the entire 0 0 1440 700 drawing. Larger width/height in the viewBox = more of the art visible = zoomed out.

Section 03

Punch in on a detail

A small viewBox rectangle frames just the centre basin. The camera eases in rather than snapping, because the current viewBox chases the target a fraction each frame.

Section 04

Frame the beacon

Point a waypoint anywhere in the coordinate space — here, the pulsing marker near the east cape. The marker animation is pure CSS, independent of the camera.

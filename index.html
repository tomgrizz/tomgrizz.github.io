Research · Acoustic Telemetry

Where do young walleye actually go?

Investigating young walleye movement and migration in the Bay of Quinte and Lake Ontario using acoustic telemetry

MSc Thesis · Queen's University · Freshwater Fisheries Conservation Lab · 2026

Twenty-nine young, sexually mature male walleye were tagged with acoustic transmitters in the Napanee and Trent Rivers and tracked for five years across the GLATOS receiver network — producing 65 fish-years of fine-scale movement data on a life stage that traditional netting surveys could never resolve.

  • Walleye as young as age 3 migrated out of the bay into eastern Lake Ontario — earlier than traditional assessment methods suggested.
  • Individual migrations were highly repeatable year over year (R = 0.88–0.91), mirroring patterns documented in adults.
  • Age predicted annual distance travelled, but not range extent or distance from spawning grounds — individual behaviour mattered more.
  • Tagged fish crossed into U.S. waters, underscoring the need for bi-national assessment of the Lake Ontario walleye fishery.
